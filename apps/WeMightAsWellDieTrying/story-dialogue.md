# Story dialogue

Every voiced line, by scene. Square-bracket words like [sighs] or [angry] are emotion tags: never shown or spoken, they set the speaker's face (and their voice's pitch) from where they stand. Stutters and ... are written into the text. Italic lines are scene context, not spoken. (pause) marks a pause the game adds before that page.

## opening (day 1, 21:40)

*A wet street with one patrol car at the kerb, its light bar flickering blue and red on the puddles. Pike sits on the bonnet, stands, sits again. The camera drifts toward the school at the far end, where buses wait with their headlights on.*

- **Radio**: All units. Chief Albright is not responding.

*A handset with a red LED. Reyes's hand lifts the mic.*

- **JEC**: Officer Reyes... Do you copy? Our on-site commander is dead. By order of the Joint Evacuation Command, you are Commander. Take control of Main Street.
- **Reyes**: [startled] W-wait, what? I'm just a patrol officer.
- **JEC**: [firmly] Not any more. You. Have. No. Choice.
- **Nora**: [warmly] This is dispatch. Congratulations, Danny. You got a promotion in no time.
- **Nora**: [nervously] I-I'm sorry... Let's get to work.

*Silhouettes stand in a long line at the buses. Reyes walks along it, cap in hand. Pike falls in two steps behind. Dunne leans on a bus and shrugs.*

- **Pike**: How long before the buses go, sir?
- **Reyes**: [worried] Forty minutes. Hopefully.
- **Dunne**: [dryly] That's what they said last time.

## after:main-street (day 1, 22:20)

*Fourteen buses pull away one at a time toward the left edge, headlights shrinking. The crowd on the pavement waves. Reyes stands still at the centre.*

- **Pike**: [sighs] That was terrible.
- **Reyes**: [tired] That's all fourteen.

*The red LED stutters. On each dropped word, the picture tears.*

- **Nora**: [sadly] Danny. Bad news. The overpass was already down.
- **Nora**: [sadly] The buses use that route. They're... gone.
- **Nora**: B-but three turned back. [hopeful] They're going round by the farm road.
- **Reyes**: [worried] How many in those buses?
- **Nora**: [quietly] Two hundred and twelve.

*The first page, written stroke by stroke. The 212 is underlined twice.*

- **JEC**: Commander, you are needed in Chicago.

## before:chicago (day 3, 05:10)

*Grey dawn. A pier with three ferries tied up, funnel smoke. The crowd of 6,000 is a dense field of tiny heads. An elevated train line crosses the backdrop with one stalled car, doors open. The squad climbs down from a truck.*

- **Harbor master**: [desperate] There's only three ferries, sir! We can start loading at six. Please, buy us some time!
- **Reyes**: [frustrated] Dammit. How long?
- **Harbor master**: A-at least... six hours.

*Nora's voice is steadier.*

- **Nora**: They relocated me to a boat. Good luck, sir.
- **Reyes**: [dryly] Lucky you.

- **Pike**: [worried] Six hours, huh. This is going to be hell.
- **Dunne**: [chuckles] Seen longer.

## after:chicago (day 3, 11:40)

*Noon. The first ferry fills and lists. The harbor master's whistle puffs steam, the dock gate shuts, and the ferry pulls away. The other two cast off, sail past the breakwater and stop, small and still on the water. The crowd stays on the pier. The camera pulls back until the two ferries are two pixels wide.*

- **Reyes**: [shocked] What are they doing?!
- **Harbor master**: The boats have to hold offshore until the pier is safe.
- **Dunne**: [angry] It won't be clear!

- **Nora**: [sadly] They're not coming back in, Danny. Best we can do is send a rubber boat.
- **Reyes**: [worried] How many got in, Nora?
- **Nora**: [quietly] Four hundred and ten.

*Nine people from the pier stand behind the squad and will not leave. A small child holds Pike's sleeve. Hollis stops at the gate and takes off his cap. In the crowd, a woman raises an arm. Hollis walks into the field of heads and is lost among them.*

- **Hollis**: [quietly] My sister's in that crowd, Commander.
- **Reyes**: [quietly] I understand.

*Below the entry, in smaller writing, the note.*

- **Pike**: [tired] Where to?
- **JEC**: Head to New York, America is falling back to Washington. You need to escort high-profile personnel out of New York.

## before:new-york (day 6, 03:30)

*A street-level canyon at night. Dark towers, a few lit windows blinking slowly. A stalled taxi. Steam from a manhole. The Column, now 14, sits on the kerb passing one canteen from hand to hand. The camera tilts up a tower to a thin orange line on the horizon.*

*JEC speaks directly, in the squared font.*

- **JEC**: Eleven thousand at the harbor plaza. The fleet lifts them in runs. Last run departs 09:00. Hold the three avenues until then.
- **Dunne**: [worried] We can't hold eleven thousand long enough...
- **Pike**: [firmly] We have to try.

## after:new-york (day 6, 08:20)

*Two lifts have gone. The crowd waits on the quay. The ships of the third run approach, large and slow; mid-harbor each one turns, flipping direction in two clumsy frames. The crowd lifts its arms in a slow wave, then lowers them.*

- **Loudspeaker**: ALL VESSELS RETURN TO STATION. FLEET RECALLED.

- **JEC**: Fleet recalled to cover another port. V.I.P.s have been evacuated. Your orders stand. Thank you for your service.
- **Reyes**: [furious] Another goddamn port?!
- **Pike**: [shocked] Did they just—
- **Reyes**: [flatly] Yes.
- **Pike**: [shouting] ELEVEN THOUSAND!

*Dunne takes off his cap and puts it back on. A child from the Column offers Reyes a piece of bread. He takes it and does not eat it.*

- **Dunne**: [tired] We can only hold for so long...
- **Reyes**: [quietly] I know.
- **Pike**: [hopeful] Sir, locals mentioned a tunnel network that connects to the port. Boats are heading to Manila.

## before:manila (day 11, 16:00)

*Monsoon rain in three layers. Tin roofs glisten. Strings of small bulbs and laundry lines sway. Lola ladles rice from a pot onto a tin plate. Pike takes it and passes half to a child at his knee. A thin cat is chased across the foreground.*

- **Lola**: [warmly] Kain na.
- **Dunne**: [with a laugh] This tastes better than rations!

- **Nora**: [warmly] Danny?! Thank God you're safe. We sent relief boats for nine thousand people. [urgent] But a horde is on the way!
- **Reyes**: [urgent] Quick! Gather the people to the port!
- **Nora**: [urgent] Go through the alleys!

## after:manila (day 12, 07:20)

*A grey ship at the quay, one funnel smoking. The gangway lifts. The horn lets out steam on 3 white frames. The ship leaves and the gap widens 1 px every 4 frames. The line of people reaches the water's edge and stops. Lola is among them, still holding her pot.*

- **Captain**: [desperate] I can't wait any longer, Commander. We have to go. They're at the wall. I'm sorry.
- **Reyes**: [angry] WHAT?!

- **Nora**: [sadly] There's only two thousand one hundred aboard...

*Okoye sets his rifle down on the quay and sits beside Lola and the child.*

- **Okoye**: [quietly] I'll stay with them, sir. Somebody should.
- **Reyes**: [sadly] Alright...

## before:mexico (day 15, 06:00)

*Pale, thin morning light. The giant flag ripples. A cathedral bell swings, each swing sending a small flock of pigeons up from the roofs. Thousands sit in blocks on the stones. A coffee vendor pours from a steaming pot. The camera pans right to left along the cathedral front.*

- **Vendor**: [warmly] Café, comandante?
- **Reyes**: [warmly] Yes, please.

- **Nora**: [urgent] Six helicopters on the way. A horde is incoming. They land in the square from eight o'clock. Mexico City is over two thousand metres up, the air is thin, and they can only carry half.
- **Dunne**: [flatly] Half of what?
- **Nora**: [sadly] Don't... You know what I mean.

## after:mexico (day 15, 13:10)

*Six helicopters, rotor blur. One by one they lift with a lurch, thin and labouring. The fourth drifts left, fails to climb and drops behind the cathedral: a single flash behind the roof, ash lifting. The fifth and sixth rotors slow and stop. The pilots climb down and show their palms.*

- **Pilot**: [sadly] We can only carry your squad, sir. Nothing more.

*Reyes says nothing for two seconds.*

- **Nora**: [firmly] We still need you. You can still save more.

*The vendor lowers the giant flag by hand, folds it into squares and puts it in the Column's cart.*

- **Vendor**: [sadly] Better a blanket.

## before:rome (day 21, 21:30)

*Night. The Colosseum fills the backdrop, its arches lit by warm lamps flickering out of step. Two cats on a broken column. The Column sleeps in a cart under the folded flag. Pike stands guard and falls asleep standing: his head dips over 3 frames, then jerks up.*

- **Dunne**: [warmly] Go sleep. I'll watch.
- **Pike**: [tired] I'm... awake, Sergeant.

*Nora's voice is frayed, with long gaps.*

- **Nora**: [tired] A train leaves the depot in two hours. It runs to the coast. JEC says there are ships at the port.
- **Nora**: Please hold the road until it's gone.
- **Reyes**: [worried] Have you slept?
- **Nora**: [tired] Not since Manila.

## after:rome (day 22, 00:50)

*A six-car train with lit windows pulls out. The Column waves. A boy runs beside the last car, slows and stops. The train shrinks into the dark.*

*A clock types on the black: 02:55. Reyes's shoulders rise one pixel in relief. A long pause.*

- **Nora**: [hopeful] They're there.
- **Nora** (pause): [shocked] Danny. The port is empty. No ships. No one.
- **Dunne**: [flatly] Three thousand people on a train at an empty dock.
- **Nora**: [sadly] They're alive... For now.

*Pike sits on the kerb.*

- **Pike**: [tired] Sir, I am tired...
- **Reyes**: [tired] We all are...
- **Pike**: [frustrated] It's always something.
- **Nora**: I sent a helicopter to fetch you. People need your help in Tokyo.

## before:tokyo (day 27, 23:00)

*A raised view of the scramble crossing in rain. Huge screens loop an evacuation pictogram (arrow, door, arrow) in muted blue and amber. Thousands of clear umbrellas in neat lines, each a thin swaying snake, all running toward the station. A small bronze dog statue in the foreground. Reyes takes off his cap and rubs his face.*

- **Pike**: [hopeful] At least it's organised.
- **Reyes** (pause): [firmly] When the last train comes, you take the children on it.
- **Pike**: [shocked] Sir—
- **Reyes**: [firmly] That's an order.

*JEC, now in the monospace amber voice.*

- **JEC**: Evacuation trains depart on schedule. Final departure 02:10. Departures will not be held.
- **Dunne**: [muttering] Assholes...
- **Nora**: [sadly] I tried, Danny. It's automatic. Sorry.

## after:tokyo (day 28, 02:10)

*A long train with lit windows, doors open. A platform clock flips from 02:09 to 02:10; a two-note chime. The doors close slowly. Pike is inside with the children from the Column; he salutes badly through the glass and lowers his hand. Reyes raises a hand halfway and drops it. The train leaves, the platform lights flicker. The queue does not move. The camera pulls back until they are a texture.*

- **Pike**: [crying] Sir, I can't do this anymore. I want to stay for these people.
- **Reyes**: [firmly] Someone has to help the people in the train.

*Static follows and the LED keeps blinking. After a long gap, Nora, quietly.*

- **JEC**: Final departure complete. This is the last scheduled transmission of the Joint Evacuation Command. Stay safe.
- **Nora** (pause): [quietly] Only twelve hundred got in...

- **Dunne**: [quietly] Pike will be all right.
- **Reyes**: [firmly] Sure. Now pick up your gun, it's not over yet.
- **Nora** (pause): [tired] Europe has been overrun... They need your help.

## before:london (day 34, 03:40)

*Fog as drifting dither noise. A long iron-and-stone bridge into the distance, a stalled red double-decker in the roadway. Lamp posts with dithered halos. The river ripples. The Column (21) and the squad (Reyes, Dunne, Vega) stand at the north end. Far across, a line of tiny yellow headlights: the convoy.*

- **Dunne**: [dryly] I never liked bridges.
- **Vega**: [flatly] Four thousand to cross. Fifty minutes.

*No JEC. Nora reads from handwritten pages. Reyes does not answer.*

- **Nora**: [tired] South bank says the bridge comes down at four thirty. Four thousand to cross.
- **Nora**: [sadly] Danny. None of the Tokyo trains have reported in.

## after:london (day 34, 04:19)

*The crowd streams across in a long shuffle; the front has just stepped off the far end, thousands wait on the north approach. Vega walks out among the crossing group to count them. A tiny red light blinks on the middle span. A clock reads 04:19, eleven minutes early. Flash. The middle of the bridge is gone and dust drifts. Nobody is shown on the span. The north approach is full of small figures standing still.*

*No reply.*

- **South bank commander**: [urgent] Bridge is down. Contact on the north approach. I'm sorry, Commander.
- **Reyes**: [quietly] I had eleven minutes.

*Dunne and Reyes stand at the north end looking at the gap. Across the gap Vega raises a hand, then walks off toward the convoy lights.*

- **Dunne**: [sadly] Just us, then.
- **Reyes**: [quietly] Just us.

## before:white-house (day 41, 04:30)

*Blue pre-dawn. The White House a pale block with a few lit windows. Sprinklers throw arcs of water over the grass; a fountain loops. The helipad lights blink amber. The Column, about 40, sits and lies among the roses. Reyes leans against a lamp post. Dunne opens a can of peaches and offers it. Reyes eats.*

- **Dunne**: [warmly] Last of the peaches.
- **Reyes**: [tired] Save it.
- **Dunne**: [chuckles] For what?

*After the last line, a silence of two seconds.*

- **Nora**: [hopeful] There's a broadcast on the White House frequency. Helicopters from the carrier land on the south lawn at five. They take the last eight hundred.
- **Nora**: [firmly] Hold the lawn, Danny. We have to evacuate the president.
- **Reyes**: [firmly] Copy.

## ending (day 41, 05:00)

*Dawn arrives in three palette steps. Everyone on the lawn looks up. The sky stays empty. A clock types 05:10, 05:30, 06:00. The sprinklers keep ticking. The camera pushes in until a paper label on the loudspeaker reads RECORDED DAY 5.*

- **Loudspeaker**: Helicopters will land on the south lawn at 05:00.

*Nora, with letters dropping.*

- **Nora**: [crying] Danny. It was... a recording. It's been looping since Day 5.
- **Nora**: [crying] I wanted it to be real.
- **Reyes**: [quietly] It's all right, Nora.
- **Nora**: [sadly] There's nobody left on the net but me.

*Upstairs in the White House, one window still lit, a small figure in it: the president. His arm comes up. A flash, a single shot. The window goes dark. Everyone on the lawn turns to look.*

*Dunne and Reyes sit on the grass with the Column asleep behind them.*

- **Dunne**: [quietly] So that's that.
- **Dunne** (pause): [chuckles] Well. We might as well die trying.
