    How it works

    • Share a file: choose it, and anyone who scans the QR code with their camera gets it. It closes after 10 minutes, or straight away with Close now. Up to 100 MB.
    • Room (the people icon): everyone who scans the room's QR sits at a table and can send files and text to the others. You let people in, and can add a 4-digit PIN.
    • Direct: files go straight from one device to the other. Nothing is uploaded or stored anywhere. The device sharing has to stay online with this screen open.
    • Both devices need internet to find each other. A free public service (PeerJS) only introduces them; it sees that two devices connected, never the files.
    • Some networks (school or office Wi-Fi) block direct connections. Try your phone's own data or hotspot instead.

    Staying safe

    • Anyone who scans a shared file's QR gets that file, so show it only to the people you mean, and close it when they have it.
    • In a room, files other people send are held in this phone's memory. Nothing is saved unless you tap Download, and it all goes when the room closes.
    • Program files (.exe, .apk, .bat and similar) are flagged, and QSendr never opens anything by itself.
    • Another device can see the address your connection comes from, as with any direct connection.

    QSendr for your laptop

    Sends from a laptop with no internet needed: same Wi-Fi or your phone's hotspot is enough, and it saves nothing on your laptop.

    Download qsendr.py

    Runs on your laptop. Needs Python 3 and one small library, installed once:

    py -m pip install segno

    Then start it from the folder you saved it in:

    py qsendr.py
    Windows app (no Python needed)

    One file, nothing to install, with an icon by the clock. Download QSendr.exe from the releases page. Windows 10 or 11.

    The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't signed by a paid certificate. Click More info, then Run anyway.

    Check it wasn't changed: its SHA-256 fingerprint (22 MB) is

    05A6841A4577448E5E1AB1148ECD3B75500C6828812D0921A34F69B43E8DC06D

    In PowerShell, in your Downloads folder, this must print the same:

    (Get-FileHash QSendr.exe).Hash
    Using the laptop app
    1. Put the laptop and phone on the same Wi-Fi, or connect the laptop to the phone's hotspot.
    2. Open QSendr. Its page opens in your browser, and its icon (the red chip) sits by the clock.
    3. Click the box in the middle and choose a file (up to 100 MB). The phone scans the QR code, and the download starts by itself.
    4. The people icon opens the Room, for phones sending to the laptop or to each other. Nothing a phone sends is saved on the laptop unless you click Download.
    5. Right-click the chip by the clock to quit, to add QSendr to Explorer's Send to menu, or to start it with Windows (it starts with the room closed).
    If a phone can't connect to the laptop
    • When Windows asks about the firewall the first time, click Allow.
    • Check both devices are on the same network. Public Wi-Fi often keeps devices apart, so use your phone's hotspot instead.
    • On the laptop, Settings has Phone can't connect?: it checks the network and Windows Firewall and says what to change.
    • On iPhone, keep the screen on while sending. QSendr picks up where it stopped once the screen is back on.
